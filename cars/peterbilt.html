2001 Peterblit 379 | $72,325

The 2001 Peterbilt 379 is a true classic of American trucking, combining unmistakable styling with the commanding presence that made the 379 a legend. This truck stands out with its striking red-and-blue flame paint scheme, polished chrome grille and bumper, tall dual exhaust stacks, and bright aluminum accents throughout. The long hood, oversized mirrors, and traditional Peterbilt badging give it the unmistakable look of a truck built to turn heads wherever it goes. Its bold exterior finish adds a custom touch while still preserving the timeless character of the 379 platform. Every detail, from the chrome hardware to the vibrant paintwork, contributes to its distinctive road presence.

Inside and out, this 379 showcases the character of a traditional long-haul truck while offering the imposing road presence drivers expect from a Peterbilt. Its sleeper cab provides practical space for life on the road, while the polished wheels, chrome fuel tanks, and detailed exterior trim complete its custom appearance. With its classic design and bold finish, this 2001 Peterbilt 379 is a standout choice for anyone who appreciates the heritage and personality of American trucking. The combination of its extended hood, tall stacks, and unmistakable cab design gives it the commanding profile that enthusiasts have come to associate with Peterbilt. Whether displayed at a truck show or out on the highway, this 379 has the presence and style to make an impression.
